The Film

Crime / Thriller

In the desert, every secret leaves a trail.

A desert escape becomes a fight for survival after a fatal hit-and-run forces two estranged sisters into a cover-up. With law enforcement closing in and an unknown stalker watching from the shadows, every choice pulls them deeper into the chaos they hoped to leave behind.
